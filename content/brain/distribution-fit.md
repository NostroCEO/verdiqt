# Channel-Founder Fit

## Score the pairing, not the channel

A channel only counts if the specific builder in front of you can execute it this quarter. "SEO works in this niche" is meaningless if the founder has never published anything and hates writing. Distribution risk lives in the pairing between channel and person, so score that pairing. Working heuristic: a channel earns credit only when the builder can name a concrete first action completable within 14 days (a post in a named community, a listing in a named directory, an outreach email to a named segment) and has done something comparable before. A developer with 8,000 followers on a code-sharing platform holds a real channel; the identical product owned by someone with no audience and no writing habit holds nothing. When the wedge depends on a channel the founder cannot personally operate, cap the distribution score regardless of problem severity or willingness to pay.

## Concentrated beats diffuse

Audiences come in two shapes. Concentrated audiences gather in a small number of findable places: a subreddit, a professional Slack, a trade directory, one annual conference. Diffuse audiences exist everywhere and nowhere: "small business owners", "busy parents", "people who want to be productive". A solo builder can penetrate a concentrated audience with a few dozen hours of presence. A diffuse audience demands paid spend or years of brand building. Rule of thumb: if you cannot list 3 to 5 specific watering holes with at least 5,000 combined active members, treat the audience as diffuse and mark distribution down hard. Diffuse audiences push solo builders toward paid ads, and paid acquisition for a product under $50 per month rarely pays back its acquisition cost before churn erases the account.

## What reachability evidence looks like

Reachability claims need named objects, not categories. Strong evidence: a named subreddit with visible complaint threads about the exact problem; a directory or review site that already lists competitors, which proves the shelf exists; niche newsletters that accept sponsorships or run tool roundups; a plugin or app marketplace with an existing paid category. Marketplaces are the strongest single signal for a solo builder because the platform supplies demand: an app store for an ecommerce platform, a design-tool plugin gallery, or a browser extension category with paid incumbents means distribution is partially pre-solved. Weak evidence: "content marketing", "virality", "word of mouth", or any channel named as a category without a specific instance. Heuristic for scorers: one named, verifiable venue where buyers already gather and competitors are already visible outweighs any number of stated strategies.

## Enterprise motions break solo builders

Some products can only be bought through sales calls, security questionnaires, and procurement. That motion fails solo builders in a predictable pattern: the cycle runs 3 to 9 months, involves 4 to 8 stakeholders, and requires compliance artifacts, reference customers, and legal review before the first dollar arrives. Nobody can run a six-month pipeline of ten parallel deals while also building the product alone. High willingness to pay at the enterprise tier does not rescue this; revenue that arrives after the runway ends is worth zero. Score distribution near the floor when the only credible buyer is a department with a budget process, unless the founder holds existing relationships (a former employer, prior clients) that shortcut procurement. The workable wedge is self-serve: one practitioner with a credit card who adopts without asking permission, then pulls the product deeper into the organization.

## Fast checks before scoring

Three questions separate real channels from wishful ones. First, can the founder list the first 10 places the product will be mentioned, by name, today? Second, has anyone with a similar footprint reached this audience without a sales team or an ad budget? Look for competitor launch posts and traffic sources as evidence. Third, does the founder already have standing in at least one of those venues: karma, followers, past posts, a seller account? Two or more failures means distribution is the binding constraint, and no severity or willingness-to-pay signal should lift the overall verdict past cautious.
